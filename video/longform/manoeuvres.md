## Cold open

> SCENE (quote): The examiner's sentence, typed as it is spoken.
> Parken Sie bitte hinter dem blauen Auto rückwärts ein. | Please reverse park behind the blue car. | Der Prüfer sagt

[curious] Picture it. You're forty minutes into your test, and it's going well. Then, from the back seat, a calm voice: [[Parken Sie bitte hinter dem blauen Auto rückwärts ein.]] Please reverse park behind the blue car.

[warm] For a lot of learners, that's when the hands go damp. It doesn't have to be. Every manoeuvre is a short recipe, and a recipe can be learned.

> SCENE (title): Title card.
> Grundfahraufgaben

[warm] In German, they're the [[Grundfahraufgaben]], the basic driving tasks. There are five, and you won't know in advance which ones you'll get. So today we drive all five, step by step, each with its checklist and its classic mistakes.

[serious] One thing holds for all of them. Slow is your friend. Every recipe here says: move slowly. What gets learners into trouble isn't a slow car. It's not looking.

## Reverse parallel parking

> SCENE (term): The first manoeuvre.
> Rückwärts einparken | reverse parallel parking | Grundfahraufgabe

[curious] The famous one: [[Rückwärts einparken]]. You reverse into a gap at the right-hand kerb, between two parked cars. The gap should be at least about one and a half car lengths.

> SCENE (manoeuvre): The parallel park, driven in three moves.
> parallel

[warm] Signal right, and stop about fifty centimetres beside the car in front, your rear level with its rear. Look all round. Then reverse slowly, until your rear axle is level with its back bumper.

[curious] Now steer fully to the right. The back of the car swings in towards the kerb, and the front swings out into the road. Keep going until the car stands at about forty-five degrees.

[warm] Straighten up and reverse a little. Then steer fully to the left, and the front tucks in behind you. Stop when you're parallel to the kerb. That's it.

> SCENE (list): The parallel-parking checklist.
> Blinker rechts | before you stop @ signal right
> Rundumblick | all round, before reversing @ look all round
> Nach hinten schauen | not only in the mirror @ behind
> 30 cm | from the kerb at most, wheels straight @ thirty

[serious] The checklist. Signal right before you stop. Look all round, a full [[Rundumblick]], before you reverse. Keep looking behind and to the side the whole time, not only in the mirror. And finish no more than about thirty centimetres from the kerb, wheels straight.

> SCENE (manoeuvre): The same park once more: watch the front swing out.
> parallel

[firm] Now watch it once more, for the classic mistakes. As the car turns in, the front swings out into the road. That's exactly when you check the traffic. Don't let the rear wheel climb the kerb. And keep the corrections few: shuffling back and forth again and again is a mistake of its own.

## Bay parking

> SCENE (term): The second manoeuvre.
> Einparken quer oder schräg | bay parking | Grundfahraufgabe

[curious] Next: [[Einparken quer oder schräg]]. Parking in a bay, at right angles or diagonally to the road, the way you would in a car park. Forwards or backwards. The car should end up centred and straight, without crossing the lines.

> SCENE (manoeuvre): Reversing into a bay, in four moves.
> bay

[warm] Reversing in is usually easier, because the front wheels do the steering. So drive past the space first, slowly, watching for people between the cars.

[curious] Stop once your rear is about past the far line of the space. Signal. Then reverse slowly and steer in, and the back of the car finds the gap.

[curious] The front follows it round. Keep looking all round, on both sides, and keep an eye on the cars parked next to the space.

[warm] As the car comes in straight, line it up with the markings, and straighten the wheels. Centred between the lines. Done.

> SCENE (list): The bay-parking checklist.
> Blinken | before you turn in @ signal
> Fußgänger | car parks are full of them @ pedestrians
> Mittig | centred, wheels straight @ centred

[serious] The checklist is short. Signal before you turn into the space. Watch out for pedestrians, because a car park is full of them. And finish centred between the lines, with the wheels straight.

[firm] The classic mistake: creeping closer and closer to the car next to you, and not stopping. If it's getting tight, stop, then correct. Almost touching counts against you, even without any contact.

## Reversing round a corner

> SCENE (term): The third manoeuvre.
> Rückwärts um eine Ecke | reversing round a corner | Grundfahraufgabe

[curious] Number three: [[Rückwärts um eine Ecke]]. You reverse to the right, into a side road, a junction or an entrance, following the kerb at an even distance.

> SCENE (manoeuvre): Reversing round the corner, in four moves.
> corner

[warm] Drive past the opening, and stop close to the kerb. Look all round. Then reverse slowly, straight back, with one eye on the right mirror.

[curious] When you see the kerb curve away in that mirror, start steering right. The back of the car follows the corner round.

[serious] Keep looking all round as you go. If someone is crossing at the corner, they go first, and you wait.

[warm] After the corner, straighten up and keep reversing, nice and slowly, until the examiner tells you to stop.

> SCENE (list): The corner checklist.
> Rundum schauen | before and while reversing @ look all round
> Fußgänger zuerst | people at the corner go first @ pedestrians
> Gleicher Abstand | an even distance from the kerb @ even distance

[serious] So: look all round before you reverse, and while you do. Let pedestrians at the corner go first. And keep an even distance from the kerb.

[firm] The classic mistake: while the back goes round the corner, the front swings out the other way, into the road, towards oncoming traffic. It's easy to miss, because you're looking backwards. Check the front, too.

## Turning round

> SCENE (term): The fourth manoeuvre.
> Umkehren | turning round | Grundfahraufgabe

[curious] Number four: [[Umkehren]], turning round. You'll hear it as [[Bitte wenden Sie hier.]] You turn the car to face the other way, using forwards and reverse. And you must not touch the kerbs.

> SCENE (manoeuvre): Turning round in three moves.
> turnround

[warm] First, look both ways. Then forwards, slowly, steering left, across the road. Stop before the far kerb.

[curious] Look both ways again. Now reverse, steering right, back across the road. Stop before the kerb behind you.

[warm] One more look both ways, then forwards, steering left, into the right-hand lane. You're facing the way you came.

> SCENE (list): The turning-round checklist.
> Nur wo erlaubt | and where you can see well @ allowed
> Beide Richtungen | before each move @ both ways
> Langsam fahren | move slowly, steer quickly @ move slowly

[serious] The checklist. Only turn round where it's allowed, and where you can see well. Look both ways before each move. And move slowly, but steer quickly: the car creeps, your hands are busy.

[firm] The classic mistake: holding everyone up. If a car comes while you're halfway round, you don't have to finish in a hurry. Let it pass, then carry on.

## The emergency stop

> SCENE (term): The fifth manoeuvre.
> Gefahrbremsung | emergency stop | Grundfahraufgabe

[curious] And the last one: the shortest, and for some people the scariest. The [[Gefahrbremsung]], the emergency stop. First, the examiner checks that nobody is driving behind you. Then you'll hear: [[Wenn ich „Stopp“ sage, machen Sie bitte eine Gefahrbremsung.]]

> SCENE (manoeuvre): The emergency stop: thirty, the command, the stop.
> emergency

[warm] You drive at about thirty kilometres an hour. Eyes ahead, hands on the wheel. You don't brake early. You wait for the word.

[firm] [[Stopp!]] Brake and clutch, fully down, as hard as you can, and hold them. With ABS, the pedal judders. That's normal. The car stays straight, and stops.

> SCENE (list): The emergency-stop checklist.
> Auf Kommando | on the command, not before @ command
> Voll durchtreten | fully, not gradually @ fully
> Danach | hazards or a mirror check @ afterwards

[serious] React on the command, not before. Press fully down, not gradually. And afterwards, switch on the hazards or check your mirror before you move on.

[dry amusement] The classic mistake here is a polite one: braking too gently. The examiner has already checked the road behind you. This is the one moment in your test when you're meant to be rough with the car.

## What to take with you

> SCENE (list): Five manoeuvres, one line each.
> Längs | 50 cm beside, full right, full left @ parallel
> Quer | reverse in, centred @ bay
> Ecke | follow the kerb, watch the front @ corner
> Umkehren | look both ways, every move @ turning round
> Gefahr­bremsung | fully down, and hold @ emergency stop

[warm] So, the five manoeuvres. Parallel parking: fifty centimetres beside, full right, then full left. Bay parking: reverse in, and finish centred. Round the corner: follow the kerb, and watch the front. Turning round: look both ways before every move. And the emergency stop: fully down, and hold.

[curious] Practise them with your instructor until they bore you. Boring is exactly what you want on test day. And if one doesn't go perfectly, stop, breathe, and correct it calmly. A small slip doesn't fail you.
