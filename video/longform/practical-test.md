## Cold open

> SCENE (title): Title card.
> Die praktische Prüfung

[curious] Picture this. It's early in the morning, and you're sitting in the driving school car. Your instructor is next to you, the way they've been for weeks. But today, there's someone new. In the back seat.

[warm] Your hands are a little damp. Your mouth is a little dry. If that's you, you're in very good company. Nearly everyone who has sat in that seat knows the feeling.

[serious] And here's what helps most with nerves: knowing exactly what's coming. So that's this lesson. [[Die praktische Prüfung]], the practical test, from the moment you hand over your ID to the words you've been waiting to hear.

## The day itself

> SCENE (stat): The test in two numbers.
> 55 | minutes in total @ fifty-five
> 30 | minutes of driving, at least @ thirty

[curious] The test lasts fifty-five minutes in total, and at least thirty of them are actual driving. You drive the driving school car, so it's a car you know. Your instructor sits beside you. The examiner, from TÜV or DEKRA, sits in the back.

> SCENE (list): The order of the day.
> Ausweis | the examiner checks your ID @ ID
> Abfahrt­kontrolle | preparation and vehicle checks @ checks
> Die Fahrt | town, country roads, usually a motorway @ through town
> Grundfahr­aufgaben | the set manoeuvres @ manoeuvres

[warm] Here's the order. First, the examiner checks your ID. Then come the preparation and the vehicle checks. Then the drive itself: through town, on country roads, and usually on a motorway or an expressway. And along the way, the set manoeuvres.

[curious] At the end, you don't go home and wait for a letter. You get the result straight away, with feedback on each of five competence areas. We'll get to those. And if you've passed, you get your licence, or a provisional certificate, right after the test.

> SCENE (stat): Two dates to know.
> 12 | months after the theory test @ twelve months
> 2 | weeks before a retake, usually @ two weeks

[serious] Two dates to know. You must take the practical test within twelve months of passing the theory test. And if it doesn't go your way, you can usually retake it after two weeks at the earliest. A failed test is a bad day. It isn't the end of anything.

> SCENE (list): What to have with you.
> Ausweis | ID card or passport @ passport
> Brille | if your licence requires it @ glasses
> Flache Schuhe | comfortable and flat @ shoes
> 0,0 Promille | and a good night's sleep @ sleep
> 15 Minuten | early, at the driving school @ fifteen

[warm] Now, your list for the night before. Your ID card or passport. Your glasses or contact lenses, if your licence requires them. Comfortable, flat shoes. A good night's sleep, and zero point zero alcohol. And be at the driving school fifteen minutes early.

[firm] Of all of these, the ID is the one that can end your day before it starts. Without a valid ID, the test doesn't go ahead, and the fee is usually lost anyway. Put it in your bag the night before.

## Before you drive off

> SCENE (term): The checks before the drive.
> Abfahrtkontrolle | the vehicle checks | before the drive

[curious] Your test begins before the engine does, with the [[Abfahrtkontrolle]], the vehicle checks. And first, you get yourself ready: seat, steering wheel, mirrors, head restraint, and your belt.

[warm] Set the seat so you can push the clutch, or the brake, all the way down. Adjust the inside mirror and both door mirrors. And the top of the head restraint should be at about the top of your head.

> SCENE (words): What the examiner may ask about.
> Lights, Tyres, Horn, Warning lamps, Engine oil, Coolant, Screen wash, Brake fluid

[curious] Then the examiner will usually ask you to show or explain a few things on the car. Switch on the lights and check them. The tyres: the tread, any damage, and where the correct pressures are listed. The horn. The warning lamps on the dashboard. And the fluid levels: engine oil, coolant, screen wash and brake fluid.

[warm] So practise it in that exact car, with your instructor. Can you show dipped beam, main beam, the indicators, the hazards and the rear fog light without searching? Can you find the dipstick, and read the oil level? Do you know where the tyre pressures are listed? Then this part is a gift.

[firm] The classic mistake here sounds too simple to happen: setting off without adjusting the mirrors, or without your belt on. Nerves make it happen. So make the routine automatic.

## What the examiner watches

> SCENE (term): How the drive is judged.
> Kompetenzbereiche | five competence areas | across the whole test

[curious] Now, something that takes a lot of pressure off. The examiner isn't sitting in the back, ticking off your mistakes one by one. They judge how you drive in five [[Kompetenzbereiche]], five competence areas, across the whole test.

> SCENE (list): The five areas.
> Look | Verkehrs­beobachtung @ observation
> Position | Fahrzeug­positionierung @ positioning
> Speed | Geschwindig­keits­anpassung @ three, speed
> Signal | Kommunikation @ communication
> Control | Fahrzeug­bedienung @ vehicle control

[warm] One, observation: mirrors, shoulder checks, and looking well ahead. Two, positioning: the right lane, and enough distance ahead and to the side. Three, speed: matched to the limit, to how far you can see, and to the situation. Four, communication: signalling in time, eye contact, clear intentions. And five, vehicle control and eco driving: gear changes, steering, smooth braking.

[curious] They watch these the whole way, and most closely where they matter: junctions, roundabouts, lane changes, motorway slip roads, bends and zebra crossings. So if you fumble one gear change, breathe. A small slip doesn't decide your test.

## Why people fail

> SCENE (term): What actually fails a test.
> nicht bestanden | not passed | the two reasons

[serious] So what does lead to [[nicht bestanden]], not passed? One of two things. A serious mistake, for example when someone is put in danger. Or smaller mistakes that keep on repeating. And the serious ones are mostly the same few.

> SCENE (list): The most common reasons, part one.
> Vorfahrt | right before left, in 30 zones @ right of way
> Schulter­blick | turning, pulling away, changing lanes @ shoulder check
> Stopp­schild | wheels completely still @ STOP sign
> Zebra­streifen | let people cross @ zebra

[firm] Top of the list: ignoring right of way, especially [[rechts vor links]] in thirty zones. No [[Schulterblick]], no shoulder check, when turning, pulling away or changing lanes. Not coming to a full stop at a STOP sign. And not letting pedestrians cross at a zebra.

> SCENE (list): The most common reasons, part two.
> Zu schnell | clearly, say in a 30 zone @ speeding
> Rote Ampel | running a red light @ red light
> Doppel­bremse | the instructor has to step in @ instructor

[serious] Then: clearly speeding, for example in a thirty zone. Running a red light. And one that surprises people: if your instructor has to step in, on the dual brake or the steering wheel, that's a fail too.

[warm] Look at that list again. None of it asks you to be a perfect driver. It's about looking, stopping, and letting people go. You've practised all of it. And if you watched lesson one, right before left is already yours.

## What the examiner says

> SCENE (term): The language of the test.
> auf Deutsch | the practical test is in German | Die Prüfung

[curious] The practical test runs [[auf Deutsch]], in German. For a lot of you, that's the scariest part. It doesn't need to be. The examiner gives every direction in good time, and the directions are short. Let's learn them by ear.

[warm] And if you don't catch something, ask. That isn't a mistake. A simple [[Wie bitte?]] works, or [[Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?]] Could you repeat that, please?

> SCENE (quote): Turn right at the next junction.
> An der nächsten Kreuzung bitte rechts abbiegen. | At the next junction, please turn right. | Der Prüfer sagt

[warm] [[An der nächsten Kreuzung bitte rechts abbiegen.]] At the next junction, please turn right. Listen for [[rechts]], right, and [[links]], left.

> SCENE (quote): Turn left.
> Bitte biegen Sie links ab. | Please turn left. | Der Prüfer sagt

[curious] [[Bitte biegen Sie links ab.]] Please turn left. Same verb, new word order: German likes to save the little [[ab]] for the very end.

> SCENE (quote): Straight on.
> Fahren Sie bitte geradeaus weiter. | Please carry straight on. | Der Prüfer sagt

[warm] [[Fahren Sie bitte geradeaus weiter.]] Please carry straight on. [[Geradeaus]] means straight ahead.

> SCENE (quote): Follow the road.
> Folgen Sie bitte dem Straßenverlauf. | Please follow the road. | Der Prüfer sagt

[curious] [[Folgen Sie bitte dem Straßenverlauf.]] Please follow the road. Wherever it bends, you go with it.

> SCENE (quote): Follow the signs.
> Folgen Sie der Beschilderung Richtung Autobahn. | Follow the signs for the motorway. | Der Prüfer sagt

[warm] [[Folgen Sie der Beschilderung Richtung Autobahn.]] Follow the signs for the motorway. Now you find the way yourself, from the signs.

> SCENE (quote): The roundabout.
> Im Kreisverkehr bitte die zweite Ausfahrt nehmen. | At the roundabout, please take the second exit. | Der Prüfer sagt

[curious] [[Im Kreisverkehr bitte die zweite Ausfahrt nehmen.]] At the roundabout, please take the second exit. [[Erste, zweite, dritte]]: first, second, third.

> SCENE (quote): Pull over.
> Halten Sie bitte hier rechts am Fahrbahnrand an. | Please pull over here on the right. | Der Prüfer sagt

[warm] [[Halten Sie bitte hier rechts am Fahrbahnrand an.]] Please pull over here on the right. Mirror, signal, and a calm stop at the kerb.

> SCENE (quote): Reverse park.
> Parken Sie bitte hinter dem blauen Auto rückwärts ein. | Please reverse park behind the blue car. | Der Prüfer sagt

[curious] [[Parken Sie bitte hinter dem blauen Auto rückwärts ein.]] Please reverse park behind the blue car. When you hear [[rückwärts]], backwards, a manoeuvre is coming.

> SCENE (quote): Turn round.
> Bitte wenden Sie hier. | Please turn round here. | Der Prüfer sagt

[warm] [[Bitte wenden Sie hier.]] Please turn round here. Don't mix it up with [[abbiegen]], to turn off. [[Wenden]] means turning the car right round.

> SCENE (quote): The emergency stop.
> Wenn ich „Stopp“ sage, machen Sie bitte eine Gefahrbremsung. | When I say "Stopp", please do an emergency stop. | Der Prüfer sagt

[serious] [[Wenn ich „Stopp“ sage, machen Sie bitte eine Gefahrbremsung.]] When I say "Stopp", please do an emergency stop. Then you drive on, and wait for the word.

> SCENE (quote): Move off again.
> Fahren Sie bitte wieder an, wenn es die Verkehrslage erlaubt. | Please move off again when traffic allows. | Der Prüfer sagt

[warm] [[Fahren Sie bitte wieder an, wenn es die Verkehrslage erlaubt.]] Please move off again when traffic allows. Notice that last part. There's no hurry. Mirror, shoulder check, then go.

> SCENE (quote): The best sentence of the day.
> Die Prüfung ist beendet. Sie haben bestanden. | The test is over. You have passed. | Der Prüfer sagt

[pause] And the one you've been waiting for. [[Die Prüfung ist beendet. Sie haben bestanden.]] The test is over. You have passed.

## What to take with you

> SCENE (list): The four things to remember.
> 55 Minuten | at least thirty of them driving @ fifty-five
> Ausweis | no ID, no test @ ID
> Fünf Bereiche | not a tally of slips @ five areas
> Wie bitte? | asking is allowed @ ask

[warm] So, the practical test. Fifty-five minutes, at least thirty of them driving. Bring your ID, because without it, there's no test. The examiner judges five areas, not a tally of tiny slips. And if you don't understand a direction, ask.

[curious] The hard part is the hours of practice, and you've done those already. The test is one more drive, with someone new in the back.
